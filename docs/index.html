One private place for media

Every screen you own, one player.

ZPlay brings movies, TV, anime, manga, audiobooks, music and live TV into one free, open-source app, without a required account, a paywall, or telemetry.

Download ZPlay Build from source

Four ways to play.

A single app for the screens, pages, stations and soundtracks that normally live apart.

Watch

Movies, TV and anime with hardware decode, HDR, subtitles and player controls kept close to the show.

Read

Manga with horizontal or vertical reading, pinch zoom, and chapter and page resume.

Listen

Audiobooks with speed, sleep timer and chapter controls. Music with playlists, quality switching and a persistent mini-player.

Tune in

Bring an Xtream-style IPTV portal for live TV, with channel search, favourites and history.

Quiet by design.

No required account

Download, open and play. Optional Trakt, Simkl and debrid connections stay yours to set up or skip.

No analytics

No analytics SDK, crash reporter or usage statistics. Release diagnostics are off by default.

Progress stays local

Watch, reading and listening positions are saved on your device from the first title.

Open by design.

ZPlay is free software under the GNU GPL v3.0. The source is available to inspect, build and improve, not a hidden service behind a subscription.

Made from a real lineage. ZPlay is a fork of PlayTorrio V3 by Ayman. That upstream work is acknowledged with the same openness that makes this project possible.
