The whole night, one place

Four ways to play.

A single app for the screens, pages, stations and soundtracks that normally live apart. Built around continuing where you left off, not building another account.

01 / SCREEN

Watch

Movies, TV and anime with 46 built-in HTTP providers, two torrent providers, Stremio addon support and a dedicated anime section. Hardware decode, HDR, subtitles and player controls stay close to the show.

02 / PAGE

Read

Manga with horizontal or vertical reading, pinch zoom, and chapter and page resume, so the next panel is exactly where you left it.

03 / SOUND

Listen

Audiobooks remember your position and include speed, sleep timer and chapter controls. Music brings search, artists, playlists, likes, quality switching and a persistent mini-player.

04 / LIVE

Tune in

Bring an Xtream-style IPTV portal for live TV, with channel search, favourites and history. Optional Trakt and Simkl connections can sync history and watchlists.

A quieter default

Yours is the only audience that matters.

No required account

Download, open and play. Optional Trakt, Simkl and debrid accounts are yours to connect, or not.

No analytics SDK

No analytics SDK, crash reporter or anonymous usage statistics. Release diagnostics are off; local diagnostics files remain yours to read or delete.

Progress stays local

Watch progress, reading position and listening position are saved locally from the first title.
